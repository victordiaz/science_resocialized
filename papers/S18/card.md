**Claim (hypothesis tested).** Doing a word search that contains achievement-related words makes people learn more on a subsequent key-press task with a hidden repeating sequence, even though the key-press task gives no reason to strive for achievement: participants who saw the achievement words show a larger sequence-learning score than control participants.

**Design.** Two-group between-subjects experiment (achievement words vs control), random assignment by block.

**Conditions.** A word-search "pilot test" with 13 target words. Six words are shared by both conditions; in the achievement-words condition the other seven are achievement words (e.g., win, excellence, competition), in the control condition seven motivationally neutral words of similar length. Then a key-press task: a circle appears in one of four locations and the participant presses the matching key as fast as possible. After a random practice block, four training blocks follow a fixed 10-item location sequence (participants are not told of it); then three test blocks of 50 trials: random, fixed sequence, random.

**Measures.** Learning score per participant = mean RT in the two random test blocks minus mean RT in the fixed-sequence test block (ms; larger = more learning). Awareness and motivation probes are collected afterwards and used only for exclusion.

**Sample.** The first 79 usable participants in each condition, 158 in all (206 were run).

**Exclusions.** Excluded before taking the first 79 per condition: participants aware of the link between the word-search task and the key-press task, participants with technical failures, and participants whose learning score lies 4 SD from the mean. In the data file these rules are already applied as the flag Include1 = "Include for analyses".

**Test to run.** Independent-samples Student t-test (equal variances) on the learning score, achievement words vs control, two-sided; report t, df, p and Cohen's d with the pooled SD, computed as achievement words minus control (positive = prediction direction).
