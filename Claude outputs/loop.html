Today we focus on one small part: what a loop that produces and verifies papers looks like without human supervision. We take a selection of psychology papers that have already been reproduced, so we hold both the original and the reproduction results. Both are hidden from the researcher agent and from the reviewers. The researcher gets only the data and a paraphrased research question, and runs the experiment bottom-up. Its synthetic paper goes to a panel of reviewers set up under different scenarios: social structures, bad actors and biases like those we see in academia. The panel gives a verdict, and everything goes back to the original machine, where it is unblinded and compared directly with the original paper and its reproduction.
