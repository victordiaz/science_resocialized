The papers are [N] psychology studies with a published reproduction, so every verdict can be scored.

We picked psychology papers that have already been reproduced, so we hold both the original results and the reproduction results. Both are hidden from the researcher and from the reviewers. The researcher gets only the data and a paraphrased research question, so it runs the experiment bottom-up. Only at the end, back on the original machine, is everything unblinded.
