The reviewer panel

Same paper, different panels

Independent

Each reviewer judges alone. The baseline.

Discussion

Reviewers read each other and reply. Do they converge, or herd?

Hierarchy

One senior voice the others defer to, as in academia.

Bad actor

One reviewer pushes a verdict whatever the evidence.

Bias

Prestige, novelty or a favored hypothesis framed into the prompts.

Every panel scores the same synthetic paper. Only the social structure changes.

The synthetic paper goes to panels of reviewer agents set up under different scenarios. We borrow from how academia works: reviewers alone as a baseline, reviewers who discuss and may herd, a hierarchy where one senior voice dominates, a bad actor who pushes a fixed verdict, and biases framed into the prompts. The question is how susceptible these panels are to outside influence. These five scenarios are examples; adjust them to the ones you run.
