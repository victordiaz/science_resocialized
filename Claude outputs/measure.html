At the end of the loop everything is unblinded: we see the original paper and its reproduction next to our synthetic researcher and our synthetic reviewers. That gives three questions. Does the panel’s verdict agree with the reproduction? How much do the social structure, bad actors and biases move the verdict? And can a loop that produces and verifies papers run without human supervision, and where does it fail?
