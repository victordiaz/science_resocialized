We are moving from a deterministic age to a probabilistic one. Collaboration between people and AI is stochastic by nature, so we embrace that and look for stochastic solutions to the new bottlenecks: experimentation, replication and, most importantly, review. Science has many bottlenecks, and AI can help with each of them in some way.
